# T86 portrait thumbstick session (docs/AGENT_TESTING.md), on the developer
# virtual controller, seed 20261103. A landscape session walks to the
# ORIENTATION row and selects it with the stick deflected across the press -
# X negative is the D-pad's left arm, move-left in landscape and the logical
# up direction that hard drops in portrait - so the turn must spend the held
# level, the pending edges, and the stick's own deflected arm together. The
# turned session then plays a round on the rotated stick table: the physical
# down reading moves the piece left, the physical up reading moves it right,
# the physical right reading soft drops, and the physical left reading hard
# drops, which is exactly the table the D-pad arms follow. The face buttons
# stay playable beside the stick - B rotates clockwise, A counter-clockwise,
# Y holds, X carries nothing - and the quit runs through the turned roles.
